Our Story

Founded on Trust, Built on Results

MW Executive Representation & Advisory was established to solve a specific problem: individuals, investors and organisations with interests in Sierra Leone often need a capable representative who can act on their behalf with judgment, discretion and accountability — without requiring their constant presence in-country.

From attending court hearings and liaising with government ministries, to coordinating projects and managing corporate administration, the firm operates as a dependable extension of its clients’ teams, reporting back with clarity and precision at every stage.

Every engagement is grounded in the same five principles: professionalism, integrity, discretion, expertise and results.

Founder

Monique Williams

Founder & Principal Executive Representative

Monique Williams founded MW Executive Representation & Advisory to bring a higher standard of professional representation to clients operating in Sierra Leone. With a close working knowledge of local institutions, government processes and the commercial landscape, she personally oversees every client relationship — from first inquiry to ongoing reporting.

Her approach is direct and disciplined: clear scopes of work, transparent expenditure tracking, and prompt communication, so that clients always know exactly where matters stand.
