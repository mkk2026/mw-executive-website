What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive representation and advisory support for individuals and organisations doing business in Sierra Leone — each engagement scoped, documented and reported with full accountability.

01 — Executive Representation

Representing your interests with professionalism, discretion and integrity. Whether it’s attending meetings, hearings, or official engagements on your behalf, we act as a dependable extension of you or your organisation — briefed, prepared and accountable at every step.

  • Meeting & hearing attendance
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Confidential correspondence

02 — Government & Regulatory Liaison

Engaging ministries, agencies and stakeholders to facilitate your business. We navigate local processes and institutional relationships so your matters move forward efficiently and correctly the first time.

  • Ministry & agency coordination
  • Permits, licenses & regulatory filings
  • Stakeholder relationship management

03 — Commercial & Corporate Support

Company administration, compliance and secretarial services that keep your Sierra Leone operations in good standing — handled accurately and on schedule.

  • Company administration
  • Regulatory compliance
  • Corporate secretarial services

04 — Project Coordination

On-the-ground oversight and coordination to keep your projects on track — site visits, vendor management and progress reporting, so nothing stalls in your absence.

  • On-site oversight & site visits
  • Vendor & contractor coordination
  • Progress tracking & milestone reporting

05 — Legal & Professional Liaison

Liaising with legal counsel, courts and professional service providers on your behalf — including hearing attendance and coordination with your appointed advisors.

  • Court & hearing attendance
  • Legal counsel coordination
  • Professional service provider liaison

06 — Client Reporting & Administration

Comprehensive reporting, documentation and financial accountability — clear expenditure summaries, account statements and weekly reports, so you always know where matters stand.

  • Expenditure & account statements
  • Weekly & periodic reporting
  • Document management & records
